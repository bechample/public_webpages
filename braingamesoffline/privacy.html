Privacy Policy

Brain Games Offline · Last updated 10 August 2026

Brain Games Offline is designed to respect your privacy. The app does not require an account and does not collect information such as your name, email address, contacts, or precise location.

Your scores

Your scores, statistics and settings are stored on your device only. They are never uploaded, and deleting the app deletes them.

Analytics

Brain Games Offline uses Aptabase to collect anonymous usage events that help us understand how the app is used and improve it. Aptabase does not use device identifiers, cookies, fingerprinting, or long-term user identifiers, and analytics events are not linked to an identifiable person.

You can switch analytics off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data. Turning it off also deletes any events still waiting to be sent. The rest of the app works exactly the same either way.

Purchases

The one-time Pro unlock is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details, and the app stores only whether the unlock is active on your device.

Advertising and tracking

The app does not contain advertising and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Brain Games Offline does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
