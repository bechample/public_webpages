Privacy Policy

The Haunted Factory · Last updated 26 July 2026

The Haunted Factory is designed to respect your privacy. The app does not require an account and does not collect information such as your name, email address, contacts, or precise location.

Analytics

The Haunted Factory uses Aptabase to collect anonymous usage events that help us understand how the app is used and improve the game. Aptabase does not use device identifiers, cookies, fingerprinting, or long-term user identifiers, and analytics events are not linked to an identifiable person.

Advertising and tracking

The app does not contain advertising and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The Haunted Factory does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer support contact shown on The Haunted Factory's App Store page.
